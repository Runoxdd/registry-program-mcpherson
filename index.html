About the theme

The Smart Registry: Leveraging AI for Efficiency, Compliance & Service Delivery

The registry keeps the university’s records, runs admissions and examinations administration, services Senate and Council, and is often the first office students and staff deal with. This year’s lecture looks at what artificial intelligence can responsibly do for that work, and what it should not.

Efficiency

Faster processing of admissions, transcripts, verification requests and correspondence, so staff time goes to judgement rather than repetition.

Compliance

Keeping records accurate, auditable and secure, in line with regulatory requirements and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Service delivery

Clearer, quicker responses to students, alumni, staff and partner institutions, with people still accountable for every decision.

Getting to McU

Directions for guests travelling within Nigeria. Choose where you are coming from.

Address

Km 75, Lagos–Ibadan Expressway, Alapako Bus Stop, Ogun State

Open in Google Maps

From Lagos

Public Vehicle

  1. Take an Ibadan-bound bus.
  2. Get off at Alapako Bus Stop, by Alapako Police Station (the blue-roofed compound).
  3. Take a commercial motorcycle (bike) to the University gate. The fare is about ₦500.

Personal Vehicle

  1. Drive past Alapako Overhead Bridge and continue towards Ogunmakin.
  2. After MRS Filling Station, take the exit opposite the station, in front of the FRSC Ogunmakin Unit Command.
  3. Turn right at the next junction.
  4. Drive straight to the University main gate.

From Ibadan

Public Vehicle

  1. Take a Lagos-bound bus.
  2. Get off at the Ogunmakin Exit Road, by the FRSC Ogunmakin Unit Command.
  3. Take a commercial motorcycle (bike) to the University gate. The fare is about ₦500.

Personal Vehicle

  1. As you approach Ogunmakin, look out for the FRSC Ogunmakin Unit Command on your right.
  2. Take the exit road in front of the FRSC office.
  3. At the first junction, turn right.
  4. Drive straight to the University main gate.

From Abeokuta

Public Vehicle

  1. Take a bus to Sagamu Interchange.
  2. Board an Ibadan-bound bus and get off at Alapako Bus Stop, by the blue-roofed Police Station.
  3. Take a bike to the University gate. The fare is about ₦500.

Personal Vehicle

  1. At Sagamu Interchange, head towards Ibadan.
  2. At Alapako Bus Stop, by the mega filling station on your right, take the overhead bridge.
  3. Turn left and drive straight to the University gate.
